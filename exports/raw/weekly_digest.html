LOTG weekly digest — 2026 season, week 5

Week 5 is when this season's on-pace standings begin and its trades, pickups and picks join the boards, so 79 of the 261 moves below are weeks 1-4 catching up rather than this week's news. The league sets a new single-season record for QBs rostered from one NFL team (4) — most in any season. This is a recompute, not a results week: 258 of the 260 other moves re-value settled history (157 on the on-pace list and 46 on teams), and only 2 reflect new data. 13 of the 261 were ties rather than overtakes.
